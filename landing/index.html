FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this legal advice?

No. The app helps you notice what to check and what to ask. It never tells you whether to sign. For decisions that matter, talk to a professional.

Is my document uploaded?

No. Everything runs on your device. The app has no server and collects no data.

Which devices does it need?

iPhone 15 Pro or later, or an iPad with an A17 Pro or M1 chip or later, on iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence turned on.

Which languages can it read?

The languages Apple Intelligence supports, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese.
