Clause 3.2
Ask about it The other side can refuse to pay for work at its own discretion, with no standard for what counts as unsatisfactory. What makes a deliverable unsatisfactory, and how many revisions are included before payment?
Clause 6.1
Ask about it For a year you can't work for any of the client's customers, which could cut off future jobs. Can the non-compete be limited to the customers I actually worked with?

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this legal advice?

No. Should You Sign It? helps you notice what to check and what to ask. It never tells you whether to sign, and it doesn't replace a lawyer. For decisions that matter, have a qualified professional review the document.

Does my document get uploaded anywhere?

No. Text recognition and analysis run on your device with Apple's Vision framework and on-device language model. The app has no server and collects no data. Reports you choose to keep stay on your device.

What kinds of documents work best?

Anything you'd sign or agree to: leases and rental agreements, employment contracts and job offers, freelance and service agreements, memberships and subscriptions, terms and conditions, NDAs and forms. Long documents work too; they're read a few pages at a time.

How accurate is it?

The model is good at summarizing and spotting common risky clauses, and every quote it shows is checked against your document. It can still miss things or misjudge them, so read the document yourself as well, especially the parts it flags.

Why doesn't it work on my iPhone?

The app needs Apple Intelligence, which runs on iPhone 15 Pro and later and on iPads with an A17 Pro or M1 chip or later. Turn it on in Settings, Apple Intelligence & Siri, and let the model finish downloading.

What does Restore Purchase do?

If you bought the Full Version before, for example on another device or before reinstalling, Restore Purchase checks your Apple Account and unlocks the app again. It's in Settings, under Purchase.
