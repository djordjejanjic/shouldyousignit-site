Summary

This is a freelance agreement for photography and image editing work. The Contractor must follow the project briefs and cover travel, equipment, and software costs. The Client can change the scope, pay within 60 days, withhold payment, and owns all work. Extra work over 20 hours costs more. All drafts and unused images become the Client’s property.

Key points

  • The Contractor must do photography and image editing as per project briefs.
  • The Client can change project scope anytime; extra work over 20 hours is extra cost.
  • The Client must pay within 60 days of a correct invoice.
  • The Client can withhold payment for unsatisfactory work at its own choice.
  • The Contractor pays for travel, equipment, and software.
  • All work becomes the Client’s exclusive property, including drafts and unused images.

Things to check

What happens if the Contractor wants to use a draft or unused image later?
Can the Contractor ever display the work without written permission, even if they want to?
Will I be notified of changes, and can I exit without penalty if I disagree?
What happens if the Contractor needs to work with a former customer within the 12-month period?
What exactly am I indemnifying, and is there a cap on the amount?
Why must the Contractor wait 90 days to leave, while the Client can end it immediately?
What if the Contractor completes work after termination but it wasn’t approved?
Can the arbitration clause be removed, and who pays the arbitration costs?
How long does confidentiality last, and does it cover my own work?

Questions to askCopy all

  • What happens if the Contractor completes work but the Client later claims it’s not included?
  • Can the Contractor be held liable if the Client withholds payment for rejected images?
  • What happens if the Client changes the project scope mid-process?
  • Is the 60-day payment window clearly defined in the invoice process?
  • Can the Contractor claim compensation for unused drafts or images?
  • What are the steps if the Client refuses to pay after a correct invoice?
