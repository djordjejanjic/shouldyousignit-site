One line:
Should You Sign It? explains a contract in plain language before you sign it, entirely on your iPhone.

Short description:
Scan a lease, job offer, gym membership or freelance contract and Should You Sign It? gives a plain-language summary, the clauses worth a second look (quoted word for word) and the questions to ask before signing. It runs on Apple's on-device Foundation Models, so documents never leave the phone.

Long description:
Should You Sign It? helps people understand what they are agreeing to. Pages are scanned with the camera or imported as a PDF or photos, read with Apple's Vision framework, and analyzed by Apple's on-device language model. The report shows a short summary, the key dates and amounts, the clauses to check (such as automatic renewal, cancellation fees, arbitration, non-compete or broad data sharing) and specific questions to ask the other party, with a clear caution level.

Every quoted clause is checked against the scanned text, and quotes that can't be found are dropped. A keyword scan for known risky clause types backs up the model in eight languages. If the scan isn't something one would sign, such as a recipe or an article, the app says so instead of treating it as a contract.

The app has no account, no server of its own, no ads and no tracking, and works in airplane mode. It is not legal advice: it never tells people whether to sign and recommends professional review for important decisions.
