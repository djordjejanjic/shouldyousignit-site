Common questions

Which devices does it work on? iPhone 15 Pro and later, and iPads and Macs with Apple silicon that support Apple Intelligence, on iOS 27 or later. Apple Intelligence must be turned on.

Which languages can it read? The languages Apple Intelligence supports, including English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese and Japanese. The list for your device is under Settings, Document languages.

What does Restore Purchase do? It checks your Apple Account for an earlier unlock, for example after reinstalling or on a new device, and unlocks the app if one is found.

Is this legal advice? No. The app helps you notice what to check and what to ask. For decisions that matter, have a qualified professional review the document.
